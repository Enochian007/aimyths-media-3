FAQ

Is this actually policy-safe?
No automation can guarantee a platform decision. What we guarantee is the opposite of what the policy targets: original scripts with hedged, sourced claims, a unique character, and QA'd output — the same standard our own channel ships daily. Policy linked below so you can read the line yourself.
Do you use stock/templated visuals?
No. Each channel gets a bespoke host character and its own background-theme rotation.

What if a video fails your QA?
